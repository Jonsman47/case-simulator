# Case Opening and Early-Game Rebalance

Implementation date: 2026-11-16
Save compatibility: additive v39 fields; no schema bump or inventory migration required

## Goals

The first session should repeatedly return the player to opening and learning cases without changing official rarity odds, wear, float, Pattern generation, or making ordinary cases positive expected value. Trading, Work, trade-ups, Business, and collecting remain viable supporting paths.

## Opening-first objective loop

Every local day deterministically selects three of six opening/collection objectives and one supporting objective. Objective IDs are derived from the day and template, not random IDs, so reloads cannot create a second claim.

Opening objectives include:

- Open three cases.
- Finish two distinct manual opening sessions.
- Open two different case types.
- Discover two new skin finishes.
- Earn 120 Career XP through meaningful activity.
- Reach five unique collection discoveries.

Supporting objectives rotate between modest selling, trade-up, and rarity tasks. Progress for cases, sessions, and discoveries uses bounded identity sets so replaying a session or reporting the same discovery twice does not add progress.

Daily challenge and claim state is monotonic by local `YYYY-MM-DD` key. Moving the system clock backward cannot regenerate objectives, restore the free reroll, or make a future-dated daily claim available again. This is local abuse resistance, not a claim that an offline PWA can defeat deliberately edited saves.

## Opening credit

Opening credit is a noncash, nontransferable case-funding resource.

Rules:

- Balance cap: **$20.00**.
- Eligible cases: cost **$5.00 or less** after existing case/event/daily discounts.
- Maximum subsidy: `min(credit balance, 35% × case cost, $1.25)` per opened case.
- Manual openings only. Automation and Business employees always pay normal cash costs.
- Cannot purchase case licenses, upgrades, marketplace items, or anything except an eligible case opening.
- Cannot be sold, withdrawn, transferred, or included in net worth.
- Multi-open uses a single quote and consumes exactly the amount applied to the completed transaction.

The authoritative economy ledger records the remaining cash cost as case spending. Credit is not recorded as income, so financial reports do not overstate earnings or hide cash flow.

Daily streak days 1 and 4 add $2 opening credit. Opening objectives award $1.50–$2.00 credit, with a maximum of $7.00 from the four modeled first-session objectives. This helps the player keep opening without guaranteeing profit or changing the reward generated by a case.

## Official odds and first-opening presentation

The old first-opening hook selected a Special Item table for 20% of fresh saves. That was a real odds override and contradicted the simulator's economy rules. It has been removed.

The reducer now resolves the official weighted drop first. The first-opening hook can add a visual near-gold presentation to a normal result, or identify an organically rolled Special Item, but never replaces the predetermined reward. Tests verify that changing the presentation hook roll does not change the generated skin.

## Opening feedback

The existing animation and transaction lifecycle are unchanged. Result UI now adds:

- A `New discovery` badge for the first instance of a newly recorded skin finish.
- Career XP gained by the opening session.
- New-discovery and XP totals in multi-open summaries.
- The opening-credit balance and effective cash cost in opening controls.

These values are derived after the authoritative reducer transaction. Animation callbacks do not award items, credits, objectives, or XP.

## Case Opening specialization audit

The existing 18-node Case Opening branch already covers presentation speed, manual batch capacity, repeat presets, collection/research visibility, safe workflow capacity, and later employee integration. It does not alter official rarity odds. No duplicate upgrade track was added.

The rebalance connects the branch more visibly through session XP and discovery feedback. Funding remains earned from bounded daily play rather than an upgrade that prints money. High-volume employee openings cannot consume credit or farm manual-session objectives.

## Inventory to CSWEAR listing flow

Owned item details now distinguish `Sell Instantly` from `List on CSWEAR`. The listing action routes to the existing CSWEAR selling panel with the exact instance preselected and a conservative 3% above-estimate suggested ask. The player sees:

- Current market estimate.
- Editable ask and difference from estimate.
- Current seller fee.
- Expected proceeds.
- Expected interest.
- A second confirmation step before escrow.

Locked or non-owned items cannot enter the flow. Confirmation uses the existing listing reducer, seven-day escrow, request ledger, cancellation/expiry restoration, persistence, and exact-once transaction path; it does not create a second marketplace system.

## Work usability fixes

### Package Delivery

- WASD and arrow keys use the same movement helper as touch buttons.
- Keyboard input is scoped to the focused active game instead of a global window listener.
- Movement keys prevent page scrolling only while the game has focus.
- Green targets use a high-contrast icon, fill, border, glow, pulse, and reduced-motion-safe static state.
- Successful delivery has a distinct confirmation flash and live feedback.

### Circuit Repair

- Every generated puzzle is a scrambled form of a solver-verified 12-tile path.
- Ports, selected tiles, powered connections, input, output, move count, success, and failure are explicit.
- Powered-path calculation and hints share the same connectivity/solution model.
- Touch/click and native keyboard activation use identical clockwise rotation behavior.
- The 24-move cap and performance formula remain bounded.

## Deterministic early-game analysis

`src/simulation/earlyGame/earlyGame.simulation.ts` runs 500 seeded scenarios for each playstyle and reports 15/30/60/120-minute checkpoints. It uses the production case/drop/condition/Pattern valuation functions. Work/trading cadence is a documented behavioral model rather than reducer telemetry.

| Opening-first | 62.14 | $7.72 | $7.09 | 1,302.45 | 22.27 | 62.14 / 57.86 | 14.41 min | 4 | $7 / $7 |
| Balanced opening/Work | 59.97 | $83.86 | $8.22 | 1,281.06 | 22.46 | 120 / 0 | 0.01 min | 4 | $7 / $7 |
| Beginner trader | 40.00 | $130.81 | $6.37 | 958.90 | 19.16 | 100 / 20 | 0 | 4 | $7 / $7 |
| Collector | 40.00 | $82.20 | $42.53 | 962.20 | 19.29 | 120 / 0 | 0 | 4 | $7 / $7 |
| Poor decisions | 37.32 | $2.49 | $41.81 | 802.39 | 14.18 | 37.32 / 82.68 | 25.84 min | 4 | $7 / $7 |

No modeled playstyle had a majority bankruptcy outcome. The opening-only style intentionally slows after its initial bankroll declines; bounded credit prevents an immediate dead end but does not erase negative case EV. The poor-decision scenario buys the expensive eligible case and liquidates poorly, producing long recovery gaps while retaining recoverable inventory value. Supporting activities preserve more cash, while the collector deliberately retains more value instead of liquidating it.

The full Career simulation also passed unchanged: 300,000 careers, 500,000 item-level openings, 95 upgrade rows, 45 employee rows, and a 781.1-hour competent-regular five-tier mastery projection. Representative production cases remained negative EV, including Kilowatt at 43.40% return and CS:GO Weapon Case at 33.71% return in the deterministic sample.

## Testing boundaries

Unit coverage includes credit caps/eligibility, cash-only ledger spending, exact-once objective identities, rollback-safe day state, official first-opening odds, solver-verified circuits, deterministic simulation, save round-trips, and existing opening lifecycle regressions. Playwright covers focused Package Delivery input, target visibility, Circuit Repair completion, the Inventory-to-CSWEAR escrow journey, and existing exact-once case openings.

Live Supabase deployment is still credential-dependent. Local/cloud serialization retains additive daily reward fields, and old v39 saves normalize missing opening credit to zero without modifying inventory, money, Pattern, or progression.
